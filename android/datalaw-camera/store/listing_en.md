DataLaw Camera is a fast camera with pro-grade controls and privacy by default: everything is processed on your device.

FEATURES
• High-resolution photos; 720p / 1080p / 4K video with sound
• PRO mode: manual ISO and shutter speed (on supported phones), exposure compensation
• Portrait: background blur behind people
• Live effects: big eyes, chubby, alien, dog, clown, shades, crown and more — in photos and video
• Full-frame effects: mirror, kaleidoscope, fisheye, swirl, pixelate, thermal, comic, ghost
• Burst, 3/10 s timer, pinch zoom, tap to focus
• Grid, level, histogram, volume-key shutter
• Filters and an optional DataLaw watermark

PRIVACY
No account, no ads. Photos and videos are saved only to your phone (DCIM/DataLaw).
